# Foci+ for macOS

**Foci+** is a native macOS screen-time and focus app built to help you block distractions, limit addictive feeds, and stay in flow—100% locally on your Mac with no account or cloud tracking required.

---

## Features

### 1. Focus Timer, Presets & Menu Bar Companion
- **Pomodoro & Custom Durations:** Run `25m`, `45m`, or `60m` focus sessions with automatic `5m` breaks and completion chimes.
- **Working Presets:** Switch between **Deep Work** (`45m` + Strict Mode), **Study** (`25m` + flexible), and **Wind Down** (`60m` + Strict Mode).
- **Menu Bar Popover:** View your live countdown in the macOS menu bar and click it for a floating popover with a progress ring, Start/Pause/Reset controls, and your daily Screen Time bar.

### 2. Strict Mode
- Locks **Pause**, **Reset**, blocklist changes, and quitting the app (`Cmd + Q`) until your active focus timer reaches zero.

### 3. Mac App Blocker & Daily App Allowances
- **Instant App Hiding:** Automatically hides blocked Mac apps during Focus sessions and displays a floating **Block Shield** on your current Space without switching Spaces.
- **Add Any App:** Pick from **Installed Apps**, **Running Apps**, **Choose from Finder...**, or type any app name.
- **Daily Time Budgets:** Set per-app daily allowances (`5m/day`, `15m/day`, `30m/day`, or `60m/day`). Once an app hits its daily limit, **Foci+** auto-blocks it for the rest of the day—even outside Focus sessions.

### 4. Safari & Chromium Website, Shorts & Reels Guard
- **Multi-Browser Support:** Works natively with **Safari**, **Google Chrome**, **Arc**, **Brave Browser**, and **Microsoft Edge**.
- **Short-Form Video Guard:** Blocks **YouTube Shorts** (`/shorts`) and **Instagram Reels** (`/reels`) while keeping normal YouTube videos, search, and Instagram DMs working.
- **24/7 18+ Website Filter:** Blocks popular adult websites at all times, redirecting blocked pages to a calm local shield screen.

### 5. Scheduled Focus Blocks
- Set automatic daily focus windows (e.g., *Morning Deep Work* `09:00–12:00` or *Bedtime Wind Down* `22:30–23:30`) that start Focus automatically without manual clicks.

### 6. Ambient Focus Audio & Break Prompts
- **Built-In Soundscapes:** Play synthesized **Soft Rain**, **Lo-Fi Chords**, or **Zen Chimes** during focus sessions with volume and preview controls.
- **Mindful Break Prompts:** Rotating 20-20-20 eye-rest, posture, and hydration prompts appear automatically during breaks.

### 7. Local Screen Time Insights
- **Two Tracking Modes:** Toggle in **Settings** between **All Apps Usage Time** and **Blocked App Usage Time**.
- **7-Day History & Goals:** Track your daily screen-time budget, live top apps, focus minutes completed, and streak—all saved locally on your Mac.

### 8. Liquid Glass UI & Color Themes
- Translucent macOS **Liquid Glass** window and specular-rim cards with an adjustable **Glass Transparency (5%–85%)** slider.
- Five color themes: **Ocean Blue**, **Aurora Violet**, **Emerald Mint**, **Sunset Amber**, and **Rose Quartz**.

## Installation

1. Download **`Foci+.dmg`** from the [Releases](../../releases) page (or the Foci+ website).
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Foci+** into your **Applications** folder.
3. On first launch, if macOS displays an unverified developer prompt, **Control-click (right-click) → Open** once.
4. Open the **Blockers** tab and click **Test Browser Access** to grant Automation permission for Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, or Edge.
